# Tari

> Tari is an open-source, proof-of-work blockchain protocol focused on privacy (default confidential transactions) and accessibility. The Tari Universe app lets everyday users mine XTM tokens, and the protocol exposes self-hosted Base Node and Wallet gRPC interfaces for developers, exchanges, and dApp builders. A Layer-2 (Ootle) adds smart-contract templates.

## APIs

- [Base Node & Wallet gRPC](https://github.com/tari-project/tari/tree/development/applications/minotari_app_grpc/proto): proto3 (package tari.rpc) service definitions for querying/submitting to the chain and controlling a wallet.

## SDKs & CLI

- [@tari-project/tarijs](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@tari-project/tarijs): TypeScript toolkit for building on Tari.
- [@tari-project/typescript-bindings](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@tari-project/typescript-bindings): TS types synced to the Ootle Rust codebase.
- [@tari-project/wallet_jrpc_client](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@tari-project/wallet_jrpc_client): Wallet JSON-RPC client.
- [tari-cli](https://github.com/tari-project/tari-cli): Ootle smart-contract template CLI.
